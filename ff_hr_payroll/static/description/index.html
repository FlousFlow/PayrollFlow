Flous Flow HR Payroll

Flexible payroll engine for Odoo 19 Community

A full payroll engine for Odoo 19 Community — the native hr_payroll app is Enterprise-only. It mirrors the standard Odoo payroll architecture so the data model, workflow and accounting behaviour feel familiar, without requiring the paid module. Built for Egyptian companies, with the Egyptian payroll taxes (social insurance + income tax) and the company document layout included.

Main Features

  • Employee Contracts with wage, schedule type, dates and salary structure.
  • Flexible Salary Rules: condition + amount (fixed / percentage / Python).
  • Unlimited deduction rules built from the UI (absence, penalties, ...).
  • Enterprise-style Python rules with result / result_rate / result_qty.
  • Hierarchical salary rules; salary structures with parent inheritance.
  • Payslips with computed lines, manual inputs (advances, loans, fines) and worked days.
  • Payslip Batches: one payslip per employee with an open contract.
  • Automatic accrual posting (Dr salary expense / Cr salary payable), fiscal-lock aware.
  • Attendance / Time Off integration: paid leave, unpaid leave, absence.
  • KPI bonuses through the native Gamification app.
  • Sales KPI / commission read from the linked user's confirmed sale orders.
  • HR Fines: record penalties and deduct them automatically.
  • Egyptian payroll taxes (country-dependent, toggleable): social insurance + income tax with editable annual brackets.
  • Payslip Register: aggregate report (PDF + Excel).

Automatic Accrual Posting

When a payslip is confirmed, the module automatically posts a balanced journal entry: Dr salary expense / Cr salary payable, respecting the fiscal lock dates. Salary rules can carry their own accounting accounts (expense, payable, tax, loan), with sensible company-level fallbacks.

Employer contributions (social insurance employer share) are posted separately: Dr social insurance expense / Cr social insurance payable. Everything is configurable from Settings → Payroll and respects multi-company isolation.

Egyptian Payroll Taxes (toggleable)

A master switch enables or disables payroll tax deductions per company (defaults are enabled only for countries with salary taxes, e.g. Egypt). When enabled, the module generates the deduction rules automatically:

  • Social insurance — employee share (deducted from the payslip).
  • Social insurance — employer share (company contribution, posted separately).
  • Income tax on salaries — progressive annual brackets, fully editable from the screen.

Toggle the switch off and all tax deductions disappear from the payslip without deleting the rules — ideal for countries without payroll taxes. The tax base excludes the employer contribution, and the payable / expense accounts are configurable per company.

Reports & Emailing

  • Payslip PDF — per employee, with the company document layout and Arabic labels.
  • Payslip Register (PDF + Excel) — aggregate report for a period, one click from the wizard.
  • Send by Email — one-click email with the payslip PDF attached.
  • Multi-company support with _check_company_auto.
  • Configurable accounts (no hardcoded chart of accounts).
  • Full audit trail via Chatter on contracts, payslips and batches.
  • Arabic + English translations included.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community. Depends on hr, hr_work_entry, hr_attendance, hr_holidays, hr_gamification, account and mail. The sales KPI feature optionally reads sale.order if the sale module is installed.
